## Tutorial - Full selection

Now that you have seen the different refinement methods, you will do the selection procedure from start to finish. You will first create your own pre-selection, and then refine it using the three refinement methods. Once you have made a pre-selection, click on the bottom right button to proceed to the refinement methods. After you have refined the selection using all three methods, you will be asked to select which of the three final selections you think is best.

For the pre-selection you can use the rectangular, angle and draw brushes to create your own selection. To make the task not too trivial we have limited you with a maximum of two selections. Ensure your pre-selection is not too small (at least 100 lines) to have enough lines for refinement.

Good luck!
