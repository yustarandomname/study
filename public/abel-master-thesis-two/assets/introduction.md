# Introduction

You are a researcher tasked with evaluating crucial satellite X-ray data. This data is essential for understanding various phenomena and making informed decisions based on the findings. Your role involves carefully analyzing the data, identifying patterns, and drawing conclusions that can contribute to the broader field of study. Each pixel in the dataset was captured with hundreds of wavelengths intensities. By visualizing each pixel as line spectrum, we can identify patterns and anomalies that may not be immediately apparent in the raw data. It has been found that machines are unable to accurately classify this data, are you able to do better?

This study is part of a master's thesis by Abel de Bruijn at the University of Delft. The goal of this study is to evaluate how well humans can classify and remove noise from a large dataset of line spectra.
